DevBrowser is a fully open-source, feature-packed mobile web browser designed for developers, privacy advocates, and everyday users. This is a vibe coded app with [Google Ai Studio](aistudio.google.com)!

Still in beta, expect bugs!

**Features:**
 - DevTools
 - Consle
 - Elements
 - CSSOM
 - Bookmarks
 - History
 - Themes with colors

And More!
